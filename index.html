Vinit's portrait · coming soon

Banker by profession, author by choice, yoga teacher by passion.

Vinit K. Bansal knows what a time-bound life feels like: he still works full-time as a bank officer. Yoga is how he stays calm, fit and creative through it. Now he teaches that same practice to busy professionals and homemakers who, like him, have to find their own hour in the day.

“Yoga is my journey of self-discovery, my path to understanding life, and my purpose to help others discover themselves and live healthier, happier and more meaningful lives.”

  • Certified Level-2 Yoga Teacher, Ministry of Ayush
  • Dietitian, with diet guidance alongside your practice
  • 14 published books, starting with the 2011 debut I Am Heartless
  • Co-author of The Man Who Became Khali (Penguin Random House India)
  • Nielsen bestselling author
  • Motivational speaker on yoga, motivation and personality development
