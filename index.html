Why choose recorded sessions over live sessions?

  • Uninterrupted practice

    No unnecessary conversations, waiting or distractions. Focus entirely on your yoga.

  • Learn at your own pace

    Pause, rewind and replay any posture or instruction whenever needed.

  • Repeat anytime

    Practise the same session more than once for better understanding and improvement.

    Follow a well-planned sequence without interruptions or missing important movements.
